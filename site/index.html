Good to know

Questions, answered

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. They open your link in a browser on a phone, tablet or laptop and they are in.

How many people can play together?

From a handful to thousands at once. The experience feels the same at every size.

Can we limit the event to our own employees?

Yes. You choose who may enter and what they must provide, such as a mobile number, an employee ID, or both.

What happens to guests' personal details?

They are used only to run your event and are erased shortly after it ends. We never send marketing messages to your guests.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. A small game for up to ten people is free, with every feature of a full event.
