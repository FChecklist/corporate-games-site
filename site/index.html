How every Corporate Game works

Nothing to install

Players open a link on a phone or a laptop.

The devices do the work

The games run on the players' own devices, so a game can have thousands of players.

Clear winners

A strict 1st to 5th and a top-ten list in every game. No ties.

Private

Players' personal details are deleted within 24 hours after the game. We never e-mail players.
