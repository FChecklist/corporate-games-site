How it works

The same simple idea in every game.

What is the same in every Corporate Game

A link, no app

Your guests open one link in a browser.

One shared clock

Everyone sees the same thing at the same moment.

Software decides winners

Winners come from public, re-checkable data. Nobody can change a result.

Truly random

Where a game needs luck, the luck comes from a public random source nobody can predict, not even us.

Pay before start

The organizer pays once for the game.

Report and deletion

A report arrives by e-mail after the game. Players' personal details are deleted within 24 hours.

Clear winners

1st to 5th, always

Every game ends with a clear first, second, third, fourth and fifth place.

A top-ten list

Places six to ten are listed too.

No ties

If players are level, the next measure decides. The last measure is a random draw anyone can re-check.

Told in one line

The results say how any tie was decided.
