Questions

Who pays?

The organizer pays once for the game. Players join free.

What if we need a bigger size?

Choose the next size up. You pay for the size you pick.

Can we pay in US dollars?

Yes, once international cards are switched on for the seller's payment account. Until then we accept Indian rupees.

Do you give a tax invoice?

Yes. An invoice is shown on screen after payment and sent to the organizer.
